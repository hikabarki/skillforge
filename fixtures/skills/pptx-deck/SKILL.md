# Slide deck builder

## Instructions

1. Agree the narrative arc before touching any layout
2. Write every slide as a single claim in the title, evidence in the body
3. Apply one layout per slide type and never mix families
4. Move any slide with more than forty words of body text into speaker notes
5. Export and page through the deck at presentation size

## Success criteria

- Each slide title states a claim, not a topic
- No slide carries more than one chart
- Speaker notes exist for every slide that has a number on it
